Everything happening across Rotman Commerce, in one calendar.

myRCSA is the public calendar maintained by the RCSA — the most up-to-date place to find RC student events and student life. Subscribe once from your own calendar app, or add your club’s event in a couple of minutes.

02 Subscribe

Never miss an event

Subscribe once and every new or updated event appears automatically in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Copy the link — don’t click it.

Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar on desktop
  2. Next to Other calendars, click the plus
  3. Choose From URL
  4. Paste the link

Apple Calendar

  1. Open Settings, then Calendar
  2. Tap Add Account, then Other
  3. Choose Add Subscribed Calendar
  4. Paste the link

Outlook

  1. Click Add calendar
  2. Choose Subscribe from web
  3. Paste the link
  4. Confirm to finish
03 Submit an event

Add your event

Submissions are reviewed and added by the RCSA team. Please allow one business day for processing.

Who can submit

Students. All recognized RC Academic Associations, RC Interest Groups, RC Community Clubs, RC Houses, and RC Affiliated Groups are encouraged to submit.

Faculty and staff. We encourage all faculty to submit postings for student-focused events.

For Fall 2026, submissions are limited to student-focused events run by student groups or RC faculty and staff. Job opportunities and deadlines aren’t being accepted at this time.
