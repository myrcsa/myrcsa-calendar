02 Subscribe

Never miss an event

One tap. Pick your calendar app, confirm, and every RCSA event lands in your own calendar — updating on its own as we add things.

On your phone

Apple Calendar Google Calendar

On a computer

Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook

If Outlook opened, confirm the subscription there. Still in a browser? Subscribe on the web — or paste the copied link into Add calendar → Subscribe from web. Outlook should show a subscribe prompt. If it drops you on your calendar instead, paste the copied link into Add calendar → Subscribe from web. Outlook on iPhone can’t subscribe to calendars — that’s a Microsoft limitation, not a broken link. Best option: add it to Apple Calendar, which puts the events in your iPhone’s calendar app. Otherwise subscribe from Outlook on a computer — the link is already copied. Outlook on Android can’t subscribe to calendars — that’s a Microsoft limitation, not a broken link. Best option: add it to Google Calendar. Otherwise subscribe from Outlook on a computer — the link is already copied.

You’ll be asked to confirm in your calendar app — nothing is added until you do.

Button didn’t work? Add it by hand

Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar on desktop
  2. Next to Other calendars, click the plus
  3. Choose From URL
  4. Paste the link

Apple Calendar

  1. Open Settings, then Calendar
  2. Tap Add Account, then Other
  3. Choose Add Subscribed Calendar
  4. Paste the link

Outlook

  1. Click Add calendar
  2. Choose Subscribe from web
  3. Paste the link
  4. Confirm to finish
03 Submit an event

Add your event

Submissions are reviewed and added by the RCSA team. Please allow one business day for processing.

Who can submit

Students. All recognized RC Academic Associations, RC Interest Groups, RC Community Clubs, RC Houses, and RC Affiliated Groups are encouraged to submit.

Faculty and staff. We encourage all faculty to submit postings for student-focused events.

For Fall 2026, submissions are limited to student-focused events run by student groups or RC faculty and staff. Job opportunities and deadlines aren’t being accepted at this time.
